# Spider-Man (2002) Movie Arc

## Overview
Spider-Man (2002), directed by Sam Raimi, follows the origin story of Peter Parker, a high school student who gains spider-like abilities after being bitten by a genetically modified spider.

## Main Plot Points
- Peter Parker gets bitten by a radioactive spider during a field trip
- He discovers his new superpowers: wall-crawling, enhanced strength, and "spider-sense"
- After his uncle Ben is killed by a carjacker, Peter learns "With great power comes great responsibility"
- Peter becomes Spider-Man to fight crime in New York City
- He faces the Green Goblin (Norman Osborn), his best friend Harry's father
- The climax involves a confrontation between Spider-Man and Green Goblin, resulting in Norman's death
- Peter sacrifices his relationship with Mary Jane Watson to protect her

## Key Themes
- Responsibility and power
- Coming of age
- Sacrifice for the greater good
- The hero's journey

## Cast
- Tobey Maguire as Peter Parker/Spider-Man
- Willem Dafoe as Norman Osborn/Green Goblin
- Kirsten Dunst as Mary Jane Watson
- James Franco as Harry Osborn
